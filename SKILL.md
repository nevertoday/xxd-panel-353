## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is one full-page engraved plate on a single deep ground pressed from the photograph's darkest colour to almost ink, keeping a hint of its warmth or coolness. A very large triangular cone of light rises point-up from the centre of a low horizon behind the subject: it is built only from countless fine, distinct stippled dots, wide and dense at the base and thinning towards the tip, with softly scattering edges, and its tip stops below generous headroom, with one tiny bright point beside it. The subject, reduced to its most representative single object or fragment, sits small and low to one side at the foot of the cone as a dark wood-engraved silhouette made of parallel cut lines whose density sets the value, with only a few bright lines on the side facing the light; the ground is a low strip of the same engraved lines, and a few long horizontal engraved bands lie across the dark field. Use exactly two colours: the near-ink dark and one creamy light, brightest in the middle of the cone and at the point beside its tip. The text is one tiny figure number followed by a one-line caption in small classical serif, cream, centred on the bottom edge of the design and well clear of the ground, written like an atlas author calmly recording a strange event and naming something actually visible in the photograph. Nothing touches the fold. Reject a full-scene copy, a filled or busy ground, a solid white triangle or stage spotlight, an enlarged subject that fills the frame, a grey rather than deep ground, any third colour or colour gradient fill, photographic realism, soft-focus glow, and messy, random dots.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-353/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
